# Die With Zero Simulator: how to use it

**The problem:** each round takes too long because cards are dealt by hand and LEP is counted on a calculator.
**The fix:** one phone runs the table. Every decision is a button, cards and dice are drawn in the app, and LEP is calculated automatically, including memory dividends and legacy. Only Dare Yourself is done physically.

## Open it

- **App link (GitHub):** https://wkahjian93.github.io/die-with-zero-simulator/ is the version to install on phones and tablets. It has its own icon, opens full screen with no browser bar, and works offline after the first open.
- **Backup link (Claude):** https://claude.ai/artifact/4D2DqCmHDHhv7gPawzePLY (currently shared as "anyone with the link"; change this in the page's Share menu).
- Use **one facilitator phone** for the whole table. The game saves after every tap, so if the phone locks or the page closes, reopen it and tap **Continue game**.

## Install it on a phone or tablet

| Device | Steps |
| --- | --- |
| iPhone / iPad | Open the app link in **Safari** (not Chrome) → tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**. |
| Android | Open the app link in **Chrome** → tap **⋮** → **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**) → **Install**. |

- The **Die With Zero** icon (yellow with a blue 0) appears on the home screen. Open the game from the icon.
- **Updates:** when the game changes, open the app once while online. It loads the new version, and saved games are kept.
- **Saved games stay on that device.** A game started on one phone can't be continued on another.
- The fonts need internet the first time. Without internet the game still works, in a standard font.

## Running a game (about 4 minutes per player per round)

1. **Setup:** choose 1–5 players, type names, and keep the 4-minute timer on. Tap **Start game at age 20**.
2. **Round screen:** the app refreshes everyone's T, applies the ageing H drop, and adds retirement income at age 70. Read it out, then tap **Start [name]'s turn**.
3. **Player's turn:** the timer starts automatically. Tap it to pause. Experiences, Buy time and Recover health can be used as many times as M, T and H allow; the other actions once per round:
   - **Earn money** (rounds 1–5): Stable job −3 T → +6 M, or High-risk career −2 T. For the career option, the app shows a dare, the player performs it, and you tap **PASS (+12 M)** or **FAIL (+0 M)** after the group vote.
   - **Buy a life experience:** each card shows the exact LEP the player would get at their current H.
   - **Invest money:** pick the amount, then **Roll dice in app**, or roll real dice and pick the total.
   - **Buy time** (2 M → 2 T), **Recover health** (2 T → 1 H, up to age 50), **Buy insurance** (2 M).
   - **Give legacy to children:** pick an amount (or **All**). Each 1 M becomes 2 LEP.
4. **End turn · Draw risk card:** tap the card to flip it. Insurance is applied automatically. For Divorce / Breakup, the app shows a dare, and you tap **Dare passed (+5)** or **Skipped or failed (−20)**.
5. **Standings** after the last player show this round's LEP and the **memory dividend** each player just earned.
6. **Final results at 80:** ranking, plus each player's memory dividends, legacy given while alive, money left behind, LEP per decade, and debrief prompts.

## Sound

Happy background music plays throughout, and every button clicks. Special sounds:

| When | Sound |
| --- | --- |
| Earn M (job, career PASS, investment win, retirement income at 70) | Cash register |
| Lose money on an investment, or career FAIL | Crying "wah-wah-waah" |
| Gain LEP (experience, legacy, divorce dare passed) | Happy jingle |
| Bad risk card (minor, critical, divorce, accident) | Sad music |
| Risk card "Nothing" | Lucky sparkle |
| End of every decade | "Ting ting" bell |
| Roll dice in app | Dice rattle |
| Turn timer runs out | Loud alarm (about 2 seconds) |
| Final results | Fanfare |

- The **speaker button** at the top mutes all sound and music with one tap.
- **Menu** has separate switches for sound effects and music.
- Sound starts after the first tap, because phones block it before then.
- On iPhone, turn the silent switch off. For a room, connect the phone to a Bluetooth speaker.
- Spending money on purchases (experiences, insurance, time) plays only the normal click, not the crying sound.

## Die With Zero coaching

The app coaches players using the 9 rules from Bill Perkins' *Die With Zero* (《別把你的錢留到死》), written in the app's own words.

- **Every action card** ends with a short book note, for example Buy experience → Rules 1 & 2 (maximise experiences, start early), Give legacy → Rule 5 (give while alive).
- **After every round**, each living player gets a coach's note:
  - a verdict such as "Living fully", "All work, no play" or "Health alert"
  - up to 3 personal observations, for example unused T, money being hoarded, a young career risk, low health or no insurance
  - advice for the next decade (Rule 7: life has seasons)
- **When a player dies, and at age 80**, each player gets a life review covering:
  - how early their experiences came
  - whether they died with zero
  - legacy given vs left behind
  - risks taken
  - one real-life takeaway
- The final screen states that the coaching is for learning, not personal financial advice.

| Rule | Idea |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Maximise positive life experiences |
| 2 | Start investing in experiences early (memory dividend) |
| 3 | Aim to die with zero |
| 4 | Use every tool (insurance, annuities) to help you die with zero |
| 5 | Give to children or charity while you are alive |
| 6 | Don't live on autopilot |
| 7 | Think of life in seasons (time buckets) |
| 8 | Know when to stop growing your wealth |
| 9 | Take your biggest risks when you have little to lose |

| Recover health (2 T → 1 H) | A big 💪 flexes 3 times, with 🥦 🍎 🏋️ 🥗 🏃 floating up, and "+1 H". Sound: two "pump" thumps and a power-up | ~2 s |

- A **Skip** link under the dice and card animations jumps straight to the result when time is short.
- The 4-minute turn timer pauses during these animations, so they don't use up the player's time.
- After a Divorce card, tap **Draw a Dare Yourself card**, or **Skip the dare · −20 LEP**.

**Undo** (top right) reverses the last tap. **Menu → Scoreboard** shows everyone's M / T / H / LEP at any time.

## Game rules (version 2, confirmed 29 Oct 2026)

| Rule | How the app applies it |
| --- | --- |
| Start | M 0, T 10, H 10. Each round is one decade, from age 20 to 80. |
| T refresh / ageing | T: 10, 8, 6, 8, 10, 10, 10. H drop: 0, 1, 1, 2, 2, 2, 1, applied at the start of each round. H stops at 0. |
| Earn money | Rounds 1–5 only. Option A: −3 T → +6 M. Option B: −2 T + dare → PASS +12 M / FAIL +0 M. |
| Experiences | **Any number per round**, including the same card again, as long as the player has the M, T and H (H ≥ Min H). LEP = base × (1 + (H − Min H) × 0.2), rounded: every 1 H above Min H adds 20%. Example: Bungee (8 LEP, Min H 8) at H 10 = 8 × 1.4 = 11. |
| **Memory dividend (snowball)** | At the end of rounds 1–6 (ages 20–70), each living player's **total** LEP grows by 10%, rounded. Example: 19 → +2 → 21. Not paid after the final round at 80, and not paid on zero or negative LEP. |
| Invest | 2–5: lose the stake. 6–8: stake back. 9–10: get back 2×. 11–12: get back 3×. |
| Recover health | 2 T → 1 H, **any number of times per round**, up to age **50** (closed from 60). H can't go above 10. |
| Buy time | 2 M → 2 T, **any number of times per round**. |
| **Retirement income** | +20 M to every living player at the start of the age-70 round (once). |
| Insurance | 2 M, covers that round only. **Not available at ages 70 and 80.** |
| **Uninsurable after a claim** | After drawing Minor Illness, Critical Illness or Accident Death (insured or not), the player can't buy insurance for the rest of the game. |
| Risk deck | 46 cards: **31 Nothing**, 8 Minor, 3 Critical, 3 Divorce, 1 Accident. Cards are drawn without replacement and the deck reshuffles when it runs out. |
| Minor / Critical | Uninsured: −1 H / −4 H. Insured: no effect / −1 H and +6 M. |
| **Accident Death** | Uninsured: the player dies. Insured with H above 3: H becomes 3, +20 M. Insured with H 3 or less: the player still dies, but gets +20 M. |
| **Legacy** | Giving while alive (button): 1 M = **2 LEP**. When a player dies, all their M becomes LEP at **1 : 1**. At age 80, every survivor's leftover M also becomes inheritance at 1 : 1. |
| Divorce / Breakup | −20 LEP. If the player does the dare and passes: +5 LEP instead. |
| Players who die | They're out, but their LEP (including legacy) counts at the end. This can be switched off in setup. |

## Test run (29 Oct 2026, phone size 375×812)

Five players, with the risk cards set in advance so every new rule could be checked by hand:

| Player | What happened | App result | Hand check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ali, age 20 | Job, Bungee, gave all 4 M | LEP 11 + 8 = 19, then memory +2 → **21** | 4 M × 2 = 8; 19 × 10% = 1.9 → 2 ✓ |
| Mei Ling, 20 | Career PASS (+12 M), insured, Europe, then Critical Illness | M 12, H 9, **LEP 26**, uninsurable | 24 + 2.4 → 26 ✓ |
| Kumar, 20 | Job, invested 2 M (rolled 11), Japan, Minor Illness | M 4, H 9, **LEP 22**, uninsurable | ✓ |
| Siti, 20 | Career FAIL, Divorce with dare passed | LEP 5 + 1 → **6** | ✓ |
| Jason, 20 | Job (+6 M), uninsured Accident | Died; 6 M → **+6 LEP** | ✓ |
| Ali, 30 | Insured, Accident with H 9 | H → **3**, +20 M → M 24 | ✓ |
| Mei Ling, 30 | Tried to buy insurance | **Blocked** (uninsurable) | ✓ |
| Siti, 30 | H 3, insured, Accident | Died; 4 + 20 = 24 M → **LEP 30** | ✓ |

The app also checked these:
- **Health recovery:** open at ages 40 and 50, closed at 60.
- **Earning money:** closed at 70.
- **Insurance:** closed at 70 and 80.
- **Retirement income:** +20 M arrived at age 70.
- **Final scoring:** at age 80, every survivor's leftover money became LEP 1:1. Every player's final total matched the sum of their per-round LEP.

**One balance point to watch:** the test players were bots that never recovered health, and they reached H 0 by around 60 with lots of money left over. Their money ended up as inheritance at only 1 LEP per M. In real games, the Give legacy button (2 LEP per M) will be the key late-game decision, which makes a good debrief point.
